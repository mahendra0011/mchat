# Orbit — Acceptable Use Rules & Enforcement Categories

These rules apply to every account, personal workspace, and business workspace on Orbit.
They exist to keep the platform safe from spam, harassment, impersonation, malicious
files/links, and abuse of business or group permissions — without invasive content
surveillance of private conversations.

## Prohibited conduct

1. **Spam & unwanted contact.** Mass-messaging, unsolicited bulk contact, coordinated
   fake accounts, or repeatedly contacting people who have not accepted a relationship.
2. **Harassment & hate.** Targeted abuse, threats, doxxing, or content that promotes
   hatred or violence against individuals or protected groups.
3. **Impersonation.** Impersonating another person, brand, or Orbit staff; misleading
   use of names, handles, or profile imagery to deceive.
4. **Malicious files & links.** Distributing malware, phishing links, or content that
   exploits viewers; tampered link previews designed to mislead.
5. **Illegal & harmful content.** Content that violates applicable law, sexual
   exploitation of minors, or coordination of real-world harm.
6. **Abuse of business or group permissions.** Using admin/moderator tools to silence
   reporting, bypass individual blocks, or harm members.
7. **Circumvention.** Evading enforcement, bans, or safety controls; repackaging
   restricted accounts to regain reach.

## Enforcement categories & proportional actions

| Category | Typical examples | Range of actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spam** | Bulk contact requests, unsolicited broadcasting | Rate-limit → warning → temporary hold → suspension |
| **Harassment** | Targeted abuse, unwanted repeated contact | Warning → temporary hold → suspension |
| **Impersonation** | Fake identity, deceptive profiles | Warning → removal of identity signals → suspension |
| **Malicious content** | Phishing links, malware attachments | Immediate removal → suspension |
| **Illegal content** | Content violating applicable law | Immediate suspension → report to authorities |
| **Permission abuse** | Admin tools used to harm members | Role removal → temporary hold → suspension |

Actions are chosen to be **proportional** to severity, history, and reach. A report does
**not** automatically imply guilt; every material action is eligible for human review.

## Review, appeal & transparency

- Reports are reviewed by authorized trust & safety reviewers only, under a documented
  retention schedule (abuse reports: 730 days).
- Anyone subject to a **material restriction** (temporary hold or suspension) may
  **appeal**. Reporters may also appeal a decision they believe was wrong. Appeals are
  decided by a reviewer; an upheld appeal reverses the action.
- We publish **aggregate, privacy-safe transparency reports** (report volumes and
  enforcement outcomes) via `GET /v1/transparency/summary`. These contain no personal
  data and never expose private message content.

## Reporting

Use the in-product **Report** control on any conversation or message. Before you submit,
Orbit shows exactly what will be shared (the selected messages and limited metadata) —
your full chat history is never uploaded by default.

## Contact

Questions about these rules or a specific enforcement decision: reach out to the trust &
safety team through the in-product report flow or your workspace administrator.
